Corvus Mail

A desktop email client application for Windows 10 and Windows 11.

What Corvus Mail does

Corvus Mail is an email client application that runs on your Windows PC. It connects to your existing email accounts and lets you send, receive, read, and organize your email in one place. Corvus Mail supports:

  • Gmail — sign in with your Google account to read and send Gmail, manage labels, and sync Google Calendar, Contacts, and Tasks.
  • Outlook and Microsoft 365 — sign in with your Microsoft account for mail, calendar, contacts, and tasks.
  • Any IMAP or POP3 provider — Yahoo, iCloud, Fastmail, or your own mail server, with CalDAV/CardDAV support for calendar and contacts.

Why Corvus Mail exists

Corvus Mail is a native Windows application built for speed and privacy. It requests access to your email through the official OAuth 2.0 sign-in flows of Google and Microsoft. Your credentials and your mail data stay on your device — the developer does not operate any servers that store your mail.

Privacy

Read our full Privacy Policy. In short: OAuth tokens and email data are stored locally on your device only, and Corvus Mail contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking. You can revoke access at any time from your provider's account security page.

Questions or access requests: use the support email shown on the app's sign-in (consent) screen.

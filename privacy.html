Corvus Mail — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Overview

Corvus is a desktop email client for Windows. The developer of Corvus does not operate servers that store your mail or account credentials. Corvus connects directly from your device to your email providers (Google, Microsoft, or your own IMAP/POP3/CalDAV/CardDAV servers).

What data Corvus accesses

Depending on which account capabilities you enable, Corvus requests OAuth 2.0 access to:

  • Gmail: read and manage your mail (IMAP/XOAUTH2 and Gmail API), labels, and send mail on your behalf.
  • Google Calendar / Contacts / Tasks: read and write entries you choose to sync.
  • Microsoft accounts: mail, calendar, contacts, and tasks through Microsoft Graph.

What Corvus does with your data

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device only.
  • Mail, calendar, contact, and task data is cached in a local database on your device only.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party other than your email provider.
  • Corvus contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Removing access

You can revoke Corvus's access at any time from your Google account permissions page or the equivalent page for other providers. Deleting the app from your device does not revoke tokens; revoking access does.

Contact

For questions, data deletion requests, or access issues, use the support email shown on the app's sign-in (consent) screen. You can also revoke access directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
